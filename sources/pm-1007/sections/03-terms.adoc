== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

=== grammar
the PARG grammar `grammars/latex.parg` of the `latexmath` gem, generated from the LaTeX grammar of Plurimath at the commit named in the clause "The grammar artifact"

=== artifact
the compiled form of the grammar, `artifacts/latex.json`, with its bindings and the checksum file `artifacts/latex.checksums.txt`

=== rewritten text
the copy of the input that the grammar reads, produced by the preparation steps

=== typed model
the tree of `Latexmath::Model` classes that parsing returns; `Latexmath::Model::Document` is its root

=== capture
a named part of a grammar rule; the captures give the fields of the typed model

=== mapper
the part of the `plurimath` gem that turns the typed model into a formula tree, `Plurimath::Latex::Mapper`

=== construct
a form of the notation, such as a script, a fraction or an environment
