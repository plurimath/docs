== Foreword

=== General

This document is the seventh document of the Plurimath specifications series. It specifies the LaTeX math notation that Plurimath reads and writes: the grammar that accepts it, the typed model the grammar produces, what Plurimath makes of that model, what LaTeX output loses, and the corpora that check all of this.

The notation is the LaTeX math that Plurimath read when its LaTeX grammar was still part of the `plurimath` gem. The grammar now lives in the `latexmath` gem and is carried by a checksummed artifact (clause "The grammar artifact"). Where this document and the implementation disagree, the disagreement is a defect in one of the two.

This document is the PM LaTeX document of the specification set tracked in https://github.com/plurimath/plurimath/issues/525[plurimath#525].
