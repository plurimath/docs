== Scope

=== General

This document specifies:

* the preparation of the input before the grammar runs;
* the structure of a formula and the constructs the grammar accepts;
* the typed model each construct becomes;
* the reading of LaTeX into the formula tree of PM 1001 and the writing of the tree as LaTeX;
* the writing of measurement results as LaTeX and what is lost;
* the errors at the reading boundary;
* the artifact the grammar is generated into, with its checksums;
* conformance, and the corpora that enforce it.

This document does not specify: the formula tree (PM 1001), the uncertainty node (PM 1002), the general reader and writer interfaces (PM 1003), the macro language of TeX, or the reading of measurement results from LaTeX, which is proposed and not implemented (clause "Status").
