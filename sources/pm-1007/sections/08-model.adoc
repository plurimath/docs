== The typed model

`Latexmath.parse` returns a `Latexmath::Model::Document` with `expression` (the formula) and `decimal` (the marker). Input with no formula gives a document whose `expression` is nil. The model has 33 generated classes; the names follow the grammar captures (`Binary`, `UnaryFunctions`, `LeftRight`, `Environment`, `TableData`, `PowerBase` and so on). Every field with its type is listed in `model/latex.lml.json`, whose checksum is pinned in the clause "The grammar artifact".

The model is generated from the grammar and is not edited by hand. The model does not specify the meaning of commands that the legacy grammar kept without a dedicated class, such as `\rule`, beyond their captures.
