== Terms, definitions and abbreviated terms

constructor::
a reserved `keyword(args)` form materializing as a single model node.

measured literal::
the `(v +/- u)` form with its modifiers (exponent, relative, distribution tag).

quantity assertion::
the `::` annotation binding an L1 kind (UnitsML 4) to a unit expression.

reserved word::
a constructor keyword, a grammar terminal; the build fails on vocabulary collisions.
