=== The process

A reserved word joins the register through proposal, collision check, and edition: the collision check runs against the registry's symbol space and the UnitsDB symbol space, and the word joins only if both are clean. The check is the build's, not a reviewer's memory. A word never leaves the register (editioned surfaces do not shrink), and a word's role never changes — a new role is a new word.

=== Why the register is small

Six words and three tags is the whole surface, because pma's features are composable rather than enumerable: the literals desugar to the constructors, the assertions ride the unit expressions, and the arithmetic is the model's. A notation that needed a keyword per feature would be a command language; this one is a notation.
