== Foreword

=== General

This document is the sixth document of the Plurimath specifications series. It specifies pma: the notation Plurimath owns end to end, written for measured quantities and first-class features. Every token is reserved or registry-carried, every form is unambiguous, every error is strict, and nothing is sugar over another parser.

This document is a forward specification: the notation is specified here for implementation, and its first edition as an implemented surface is its first release. Until that release pma carries no compatibility obligation; from it, the surface is editioned and change-controlled like every notation in this series. The grammar is carried by a checksummed artifact generated from the shared registry (clause 13), in the artifact pattern the asciiunits content language established.
