== Conformance

=== General

Conformance to this document is the implementation of the pma reader per clauses 5 to 12, with the artifact discipline of clause 13. The claims follow PM 1003's shape.

=== The corpora

pma's own corpus covers: every constructor with its argument orders and omissions; every literal form and its desugar; every assertion, matching and gated; every rejected form as a refusal vector; the difference forms; and the strict error table. The shared corpora — the uncertainty, conversion and conformance sets of the testsuite — pin the cross-runtime and cross-notation behaviour: a pma-written uncertainty and its AsciiMath-written twin render identically everywhere.

=== Claims

A claim names the artifact checksum and the corpus generation. An implementation against a moved artifact is a different claim; the discipline is the series', and pma's forward-specification status ends at its first release, when the first generation freezes.
