== Annex D: worked examples

=== A measurement statement

`(10.3 +/- 0.2) unit(m)` — the canonical literal with its trailing unit.

. the literal desugars to `ustd(10.3, 0.2)`;
. the trailing unit scopes both fields;
. materialisation: one uncertainty node, category standard, value `10.3`, magnitude `0.2`, unit the metre.

*Expected outcome.* rendered to AsciiMath: `ustd(10.3, 0.2) "unitsml(m)"`; to LaTeX: `10.3(0.2) m`; to every surface per PM 1002 — one statement, six encodings, no per-format authorship.

=== A relative statement

`(5.0 +/- 1%) unit(V)` — one percent of five volts.

. the relative magnitude computes at materialisation: exact decimal `0.05`;
. the record is the absolute form.

*Expected outcome.* the tree carries magnitude `0.05`; the surfaces render `5.0(0.05) V` shapes; nothing recomputes the percentage at render or evaluation time.

=== A shared-exponent statement

`(3.85 +/- 0.23)e4 unit(m)`.

. the exponent shifts both: `38500` and `2300`, digit movement only;
. the desugared record is `ustd(38500, 2300) unit(m)`.

*Expected outcome.* the exact decimals of the shifted form; no rounding anywhere in the shift.

=== A tagged statement

`(0.5 +/- 0.01) ~rect unit(V)`.

. the tag attaches to the literal: the uncertainty's distribution family is the rectangular;
. the node carries the tag as metadata; arithmetic is unchanged in this edition.

*Expected outcome.* the tree with the tag; the surfaces render the tag where their encoding carries it (per PM 1002's statements) and drop it as presentation where they do not.

=== An asserted unit

`unit(Bq)::frequency`.

. the assertion names the kind; the static semantics check the dimension;
. the registry's assignment for `Bq` is the becquerel's own quantity — and the gating refuses the frequency claim.

*Expected outcome.* a materialisation error naming the gating: the becquerel is not a hertz, and the assertion said it was. `unit(Bq)::activity` — the kind the registry assigns — materialises with the assertion recorded.

=== A difference

`diff(5 unit(degC))` — the temperature rise.

. the constructor types the quantity as a difference;
. the conversion to a kelvin difference is factor-only: `5 K` of difference, no offset.

*Expected outcome.* the typed difference; the arithmetic that combines it with absolutes follows the invariants; the evaluation that converts it applies no offset — the typing carried the truth the author stated.
