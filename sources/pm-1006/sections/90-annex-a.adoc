The optional probability is omitted without an empty position, as in AsciiMath. Every refusal names its field and its rule; the message shapes are this document's and are frozen in the error corpus.

=== The constructors' composition

Constructors compose through the arithmetic: `uexp(10.0, 0.3, 2, 0.95) unit(cm) + uexp(4.1, 0.2, 2) unit(cm)` is an addition of two expanded uncertainties — valid syntax, materialised nodes, and an evaluation question (document 1004) rather than a notation question. The notation carries what the author stated; the evaluator answers what it means.
