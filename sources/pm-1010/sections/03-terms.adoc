== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

=== expression language
the closed grammar of this document: its constructs, types and errors, stated completely

=== grammar artifact
the checksummed compiled output of the PARG grammar for one runtime

=== typed AST
the parse result: a tree whose nodes carry their constructs and their static types

=== static type-check
the pass that reports scale and dimension violations before evaluation

=== exact-decimal literal
a decimal literal whose trailing digits are preserved as stated (`2.10`)

=== undefined-aware literal
the literal form for an explicitly undefined value, distinct from zero and from an error

=== round trip
parse → render → parse: the property that rendering an AST to a notation format and re-parsing yields an equal AST
