== The round-trip contract

=== Parse, render, parse

Rendering the typed AST to any notation format and re-parsing the render yields an AST equal to the original, for every expression in the corpus. The round-trip is the preservation contract: exact decimals, quoted units, uncertainty markers and the undefined literal survive every format the series carries.

=== The lossy formats, stated

Where a notation cannot carry a construct, the render states the loss (the format's interface document of PM 1003 carries the per-format statements); the round-trip claim applies to the formats that carry the construct, and the corpus marks each vector with the formats it round-trips.

[example]
`2.10 + x` rendered to the formats and re-parsed.
*Expected outcome.* the trailing zero survives the render and the re-parse in every round-tripping format; the corpus asserts the AST equality per format.

=== Reading the pinned evidence

The supporting generation and row identifiers are listed in clause 10.
Its per-target `lossless`, loss and refusal fields delimit the demonstrated
round trips. They neither establish all new expression constructs in every
format nor establish preservation after editing in an external document
application.
