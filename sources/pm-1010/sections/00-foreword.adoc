== Foreword

=== General

This document is the tenth document of the Plurimath specifications series. It specifies the expression language: one closed grammar — arithmetic, comparisons, boolean logic with implication, conditionals, iteration and aggregates — compiled through the PARG toolchain into one checksummed artifact per runtime, parsing to one typed AST that renders back to every notation format the series carries.

The grammar extends the arithmetic precedence chain of PM 1004 with the logical levels above comparison, and carries the quantity types of PM 1009 as its operand types. It is the grammar the mandate names `expressions.parg`: one source, every runtime, no hand-written precedence climbing.
