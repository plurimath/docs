== Conventions

=== General

Expressions are written in the ordinary notation: `x^2 + 1`, `(a and b) implies c`, `mean_of(series)`. The operator table of Annex A states every operator with its argument types, result type, precedence level and error behaviour.

=== Examples

Examples carry an *Expected outcome* paragraph stating the parse, the type-check result, or the round-trip outcome. Grammar excerpts are in PARG form as compiled.
