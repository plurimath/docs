== Conformance

=== The conformance class

An implementation conforms to this document when it parses exactly the constructs of clause 5 through a checksummed artifact of the PARG grammar, states the precedence chain of clause 7 unchanged, reports the static diagnostics of clause 8 with their carried data, and satisfies the round-trip contract of clause 9 over the corpus.

=== The acceptance vectors

* the exact-decimal, quoted-unit and undefined literals parse and round-trip per clause 5;
* the precedence chain parses the Annex A examples to the stated trees;
* every static diagnostic of the corpus carries the node and the expected types;
* the artifact checksums match the published artifacts, per runtime.

=== Coverage boundary

This generation supplies existing uncertainty-markup round trips. It does
not establish conformance of the complete expression language: it is not a
frozen `expressions.parg` source-to-AST generation for boolean logic,
conditionals, iterators, undefined values or the complete static type pass.
Those constructs still require the grammar artifact and the `.pgtest`
generation of clause 6. No checksum or row identifier for such a generation
is asserted here. Supporting arithmetic and serialized diagnostics are
pinned by PM 1013; they do not replace static-language diagnostics.

The repository checks are `ruby scripts/validate.rb`,
`ruby scripts/validate-stages.rb` and
`python3 scripts/validate-evaluation-profiles.py`. These validate corpus
structure and seals. An implementation's parse/write/read replay is separate
and must report the generation and the target outcomes it actually checks.
