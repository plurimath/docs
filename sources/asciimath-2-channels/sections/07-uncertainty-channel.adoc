== The uncertainty channel

Rendering an `Uncertainty` node (Plurimath 1002) into this dialect emits the quoted form:

[listing]
"ustd(10.3, 0.2)"        "uexp(10.0, 0.3, 2, 0.95)"
"ucov(10.0, 9.7, 10.3, 0.95, shortest)"

Reading decodes recognized forms back into the model with every field of the Plurimath 1002 table; the unit rides the `"unitsml(...)"` channel when UnitsML-writable. This channel is the ENTIRE uncertainty capability of the dialect — there is no bareword form.

[example]
*Expected outcome.* `"ustd(10.3, 0.2)"` round-trips category, value, magnitude and attached unit byte-identically; `"ustd(10.3)"` raises `InvalidUncertainty` (missing magnitude) instead of guessing.
