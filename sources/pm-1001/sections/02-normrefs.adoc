== Normative references

The following documents are referred to in the text in such a way that their content applies to the construction of this model.

* MathML 4, W3C Recommendation. The `Math::Function::Intent` and `Math::Function::Semantics` nodes carry the MathML 4 `intent` and `semantics` elements; the MathML rendering surface targets MathML 4.
* ECMA-376, Office Open XML File Formats, Part 1. The OMML rendering surface produces DrawingML mathematics (`m:` namespace) as defined there.
* Unicode Technical Report #28, Unicode Support for Mathematics (the UnicodeMath linear format). The UnicodeMath surface reads and writes it.
* JCGM 200:2012, International vocabulary of metrology — Basic and general concepts and associated terms (VIM). Used by the uncertainty document (1002) for the terms carried by uncertainty nodes.
* UnitsML 1002 (numeric contract) and UnitsML 1005 (XML usage) of the UnitsML series, for the contract of the `Unitsml` node.
