== Conformance

=== The conformance class

An implementation conforms to this document when it evaluates constraints under both stated rules, produces the three verdict kinds with the rule and the signed residual carried, evaluates the guards through the PM 1011 path, renders the trace per clause 9, and replays the corpus vectors identically.

=== The acceptance vectors

* the R 60 MPE ranges — each with the stated rule, verdict and residual;
* the R 134 regime laws — the guards and the applicability records;
* the series creeps — the residuals against the creep limits;
* the boundary cases — the exact-edge comparisons per the accuracy classes;
* the conditional-zone cases — the band-edge vectors per the stated k.

These rows exercise the decision rules on stated tolerances: the boundary cases (`simple-upper-limit`, `three-zone-inner-upper`, `three-zone-outer-lower`, `three-zone-one-sided-open-lower`), the conditional-zone cases (`guarded-acceptance-inner-upper`, `guarded-acceptance-outside-inner`, `three-zone-past-inner-upper`, `three-zone-past-outer-lower`) and 17 producer rows (`producer-09` to `producer-25`). Each row carries the rule, the decision, the lower and upper residuals and the guard band. They are rule vectors on stated numbers, not regulation limits.

=== Known gaps at the pinned commit

* *The R 60 MPE ranges, the R 134 regime laws and the series creeps are not yet pinned.* No row of the UnitsML or Plurimath testsuites carries them; the acceptance vectors of the first three items of clause 10 wait for an authoritative source with its edition, clause and rights. Annexes A and B describe them for when they arrive.
* The evaluator behind the pinned rows takes the guard band from a guard ratio given separately from the coverage factor (rows with coverage factor 2 and guard ratio 1 exist), where clause 6 states the guard band as `k·U`. It also implements the rules `three_zone`, `guarded_rejection` and `iso14253_style`, which clause 6 does not name. Which of the two is normative is an open decision of this document.
