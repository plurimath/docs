== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the terms and definitions of PM 1011 apply, and the following apply.

=== limit
the stated boundary expression with its comparison direction and its unit

=== constraint evaluation
one evaluation of a limit against a result under one decision rule, yielding one verdict

=== verdict
the result of a constraint evaluation: satisfied, not satisfied, or conditional, with the rule and the residual

=== signed residual
the distance from the result to the limit boundary in the limit's unit — signed to the satisfied side

=== guard band
the interval k·U subtracted from the limit boundary, per the guarded acceptance rule

=== conditional
the verdict kind for a result inside the guard band: neither satisfied nor not satisfied — the rule does not decide

=== applicability guard
the `when` expression that states whether the constraint applies to the current scope
