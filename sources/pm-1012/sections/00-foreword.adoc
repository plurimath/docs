== Foreword

=== General

This document is the twelfth document of the Plurimath specifications series. It specifies constraint evaluation: the evaluation of a stated limit against a measured or computed result, the decision rules that turn the comparison into a verdict, the signed residual that is the verdict's evidence, the applicability guards, and the rendering of the whole evaluation trace.

The document builds on PM 1011: the constraint's operands are the propagated quantities of PM 1011, the uncertainty that feeds the guard band is PM 1011's expanded uncertainty, and the boundary exactness is PM 1004's accuracy-class discipline.
