== Coverage of UTN 28

The constructs are taken from UTN 28 version 3.2 (April 13, 2023). The mapping from construct to corpus rows is `docs/utn28-coverage.yml` of the `umath` gem. Its script `scripts/utn28_coverage.rb` parses every mapped row, checks that its outcome is the one the row expects, and writes the table in `docs/unicodemath.adoc` of the `umath` gem; with `--check` it fails when the table is out of date. The table is not copied here, because it is regenerated from the corpus: the full table is the one in that file at the commit named in the clause "The grammar artifact".

The table lists 82 constructs of UTN 28 version 3.2. Of the 82 constructs, 25 are covered, 1 is refused and 56 are untested.

* Covered means that every row listed for the construct parses to the model the row expects.
* Refused means that the rows give input UTN 28 defines and that the grammar refuses it on purpose. The one such construct is the alignment mark `&` outside a matrix, `a&=b` (UTN 28 section 3.23).
* Untested means that no row exercises the construct, so the corpus does not show whether the grammar reads it. An untested construct may still be read by the grammar.

Rows that give incomplete input, such as a trailing `/` or an unclosed bracket, are refused by the grammar; they are listed with their construct and do not change its status.
