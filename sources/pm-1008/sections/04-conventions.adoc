== Conventions

=== General

UnicodeMath text is shown verbatim in monospace. Model classes and fields are named as the `umath` gem names them. Formula-tree nodes are named in the terms of PM 1001. AsciiMath is used in examples to show a formula tree, in the notation of PM 1005. The format name of the reader is `:unicode`; the writer method is `to_unicodemath`.

=== Provenance of the content

The notation clauses are taken from `docs/unicodemath.adoc` of the `umath` gem, and the clauses on reading, writing, measurement results, round trips and errors from `docs/unicodemath.adoc` of the `plurimath` gem. The exact commits are named in the clause "The grammar artifact". Examples marked as produced were run at those commits.

=== Status

Each part of the behaviour is stated as implemented or proposed. A part that is not stated as proposed is implemented at the pinned commits.
