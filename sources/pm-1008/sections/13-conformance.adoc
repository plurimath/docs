== Conformance

=== General

Conformance to this document is the implementation of the UnicodeMath reader per the clauses on preparation, structure and the typed model, and of the reader and writer per the clauses on Plurimath behaviour and on measurement results, against the artifact pinned in the clause "The grammar artifact".

=== Conformance classes

.Conformance classes
[cols="1,3,3", options="headers"]
|===
|Class |Licenses the implementation to claim |Replay requirements

|read-unicodemath
|reads the notation of this document and refuses what the grammar refuses
|the `corpus/unicode/` rows of the testsuite, and the `stages/unicode/` generation pinned by `spec/fixtures/testsuite-pin.yml` of the `umath` gem

|write-unicodemath
|writes UnicodeMath from the formula tree
|the `unicodemath` target of every `corpus/*/` case, byte for byte

|roundtrip-unicodemath
|writes a formula as UnicodeMath and reads it back, with the lossless, lossy and refused cases of the roundtrip generation
|the `stages/roundtrip/` generation pinned by `spec/fixtures/roundtrip-pin.yml` of the `plurimath` gem
|===

=== The corpora

* `spec/model/` of the `umath` gem checks construction of the model and its shape.
* `spec/corpus/replay_corpus_spec.rb` and `spec/stage_cases/` of the `umath` gem replay the stage corpus generation pinned in `spec/fixtures/testsuite-pin.yml` (plurimath-testsuite `stages/unicode/`, commit `85c7be8fd56f0cc64b4869e74716acc4f0dbe746`, generation 4, 72 cases).
* The oracle specs of the `umath` gem compare the raw tree with captures of the Plurimath parser.
* `spec/conformance/shared_corpus_spec.rb` of the `plurimath` gem replays every `corpus/` case, comparing each target byte for byte. At testsuite commit `337262eda0483ceaa43a02cc52362c5b721e5eb3` these are 20 `corpus/unicode/` inputs read (groups `fences`, `numbers`, `operators`, `symbols`), 52 `corpus/unicode/rejections.yaml` inputs refused, and 780 `corpus/*/` cases with a `unicodemath` output in 74 payloads.
* `spec/conformance/stage_roundtrips_spec.rb` of the `plurimath` gem replays the roundtrip generation pinned in `spec/fixtures/roundtrip-pin.yml`: `stages/roundtrip/generations/000001/payloads/` has 46 cases written as `unicode` and read back, of which 33 are lossless, 9 lossy and 4 refused.
* `spec/plurimath/unicode_math/uncertainty_spec.rb`, `spec/plurimath/unicode_math/uncertainty_units_spec.rb` and `spec/plurimath/fixtures/uncertainty/contract.yml` of the `plurimath` gem check the marker and units.
* The `stages/unicode/` generations are replayed by the `umath` gem, not by the `plurimath` gem.

=== Claims

A claim names the artifact checksum of the clause "The grammar artifact" and the testsuite commit and generation. An implementation against a moved artifact makes a different claim.
