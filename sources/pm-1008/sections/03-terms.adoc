== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

=== grammar
the PARG grammar `grammars/umath.parg` of the `umath` gem, generated from the legacy UnicodeMath grammar of Plurimath at the commit named in the Foreword

=== artifact
the compiled form of the grammar, `artifacts/umath.json`, with its bindings and the checksum file `artifacts/umath.checksums.txt`

=== rewritten text
the copy of the input that the grammar reads, produced by the preparation steps

=== typed model
the tree of `Umath::Model` classes that parsing returns; `Umath::Model::Document` is its root

=== capture
a named part of a grammar rule; the captures give the fields of the typed model

=== mapper
the part of the `plurimath` gem that turns the typed model into a formula tree, `Plurimath::UnicodeMath::Mapper`

=== intent form
the form `ⓘ("marker"visible)` that carries a measurement result

=== argument form
the form `ⓐ(unit ...)` that carries a unit
