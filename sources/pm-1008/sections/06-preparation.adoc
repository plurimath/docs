== Preparation of the input

The grammar reads a rewritten copy of the input. The steps are specified in full in the parser contract of the `umath` gem. In short:

* A `#` starts an equation label. The text after the last `#` becomes the document's `label`; a `#` inside `✎(...)` does not count.
* Every non-ASCII character becomes a hexadecimal character reference, so `α` reaches the grammar as `&#x3b1;`. `&` and `"` stay as they are.
* Everything from the first `⫷` to the last `⫸` is a comment and is removed.
* `\\` becomes `\`, and `\uXXXX` escapes become the character they name.
* Leading and trailing whitespace is removed.
