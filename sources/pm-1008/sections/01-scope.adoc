== Scope

=== General

This document specifies:

* the preparation of the input before the grammar runs;
* the structure of a formula and the constructs the grammar accepts;
* the typed model each construct becomes;
* the coverage of UTN 28 as the corpus shows it;
* the reading of UnicodeMath into the formula tree of PM 1001 and the writing of the tree as UnicodeMath;
* the carrying of measurement results with `ⓘ` and of units with `ⓐ`, and what does not survive a round trip;
* the errors at the reading boundary;
* the artifact the grammar is generated into, with its checksums;
* conformance, and the corpora that enforce it.

This document does not specify: UTN 28 itself, the formula tree (PM 1001), the uncertainty node and its marker grammar (PM 1002), or the general reader and writer interfaces (PM 1003).
