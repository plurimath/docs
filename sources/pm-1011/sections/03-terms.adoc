== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the terms and definitions of PM 1004 apply, and the following apply.

=== quantity evaluation
an evaluation whose operands and results are quantity values of PM 1009

=== sensitivity coefficient
the partial derivative of the evaluated expression with respect to one input quantity, evaluated at the bound point

=== combined standard uncertainty
the standard uncertainty of the result, propagated from the input components under clause 6

=== LPU-adequacy verdict
the first-class result stating whether the law of propagation of uncertainty is validated for the evaluation, or that MCM must be used instead

=== GUF₂ mode
the second-order evaluation mode: the corrected estimate and the second-order correction to the combined standard uncertainty, for declared-normal inputs

=== series
the ordered quantity values with an optional index axis, per clause 8

=== serialized evaluation
the pure-function interface of clause 10: the request in, the response out, no environment
