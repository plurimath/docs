== Foreword

=== General

This document is the eleventh document of the Plurimath specifications series. It specifies evaluation processing: the unit-aware evaluation of quantity-typed trees, the propagation of uncertainty under GUM, the series aggregations that close the type-A loop, the second-order modes the 2026 amendment introduces, and the error extensions that carry the new refusals.

This document extends PM 1004 and subsumes it: every contract PM 1004 states — the strict error contract, the determinism, the untouched tree, the accuracy classes — holds here unchanged for the arithmetic core, and the extensions below state what PM 1004's clause 6 refusal reserved for this edition: the evaluation of uncertainty nodes and of quantities.
