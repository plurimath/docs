== The evaluation, extended

=== The binding domain, extended

PM 1004 binds names to real numbers. This document extends the binding values to the quantity values of PM 1009, the booleans, and the series of clause 8 — and the binding validation extends with them, per PM 1004's entry-validation discipline: a value outside the stated types raises `InvalidBindingError` carrying the key and the value, before the tree is walked.

[example]
Binding `V → 1,2 V` (a quantity with a standard uncertainty) and `series → 5 observations`.
*Expected outcome.* accepted; the names bind the quantity and the series.

=== The uncertainty node evaluates

PM 1004's clause 6 refusal — an uncertainty node raises `UnsupportedExpressionError` — is lifted by this document. The evaluation of an uncertainty node yields its quantity per PM 1009: the node's category, fields and exact decimals as PM 1002 states them, carried unchanged. The *propagation* of uncertainties through operations is clause 6.

=== The precedence chain, extended

The evaluation follows the grammar's chain of PM 1010: the logical levels above the comparison level PM 1004 pins. The arithmetic core folds as PM 1004 states; the logical operators evaluate per their truth tables over the booleans; the conditional evaluates its condition and exactly one branch.

=== What is still refused

A `Text` node, a matrix, and every construct outside PM 1010's grammar still raise `UnsupportedExpressionError`. The refusal shrinks to exactly what remains undefined; it does not disappear.
