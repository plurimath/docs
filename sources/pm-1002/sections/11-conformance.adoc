== Conformance

=== General

Conformance to this document is the implementation of the uncertainty node per clause 5 with the encodings of clause 9. Claims are per class; every claim is discharged by replaying frozen vectors.

=== The uncertainty corpus

The corpus is a frozen set of nodes with, per format, their expected encodings. Entries state their provenance (the node's fields, the model version that froze the encodings) and replay without modification. Encodings change only at an edition of this document.

=== Round-trip classes

A claim of round-trip for a format means: reading the format's encoding of a corpus node back yields a node equal to it. Round-trip claims exist for the formats whose readers are specified in document 1003; this document defines the property, that document its mechanics.

=== Corpus governance

The uncertainty corpus is owned like every corpus in the series: entries are frozen with provenance, replay byte-identically, and change only at an edition. A change to any encoding of this document — a punctuation move, a statement rewording, a marker change — produces a model-version change and a new corpus generation, never a patch over frozen bytes.

The corpus covers, at minimum: every category in every format; the concise and absolute notations; the two interval kinds; optional fields present and absent; the unit expressible and not expressible; and every refusal vector of Annex C.

=== Drift and disagreement

An implementation whose encodings differ from the corpus does not conform at the corpus's model version. An implementation whose tree differs from the clause 5 model does not conform, whatever its encodings do. Disagreements between this document and the implementation are resolved by fixing one of the two and recording which moved.
