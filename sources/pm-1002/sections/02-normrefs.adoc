== Normative references

The following documents are referred to in the text in such a way that their content applies to the construction of this model.

* JCGM 100:2008 + Amd.1:2026, _Evaluation of measurement data — Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement (GUM)_. Clause 6.2.1 and 7.2.3 (expanded uncertainty and its coverage factor), 7.2.1 and 7.2.2 (standard uncertainty and the reporting forms), and Annex G.
* JCGM 101:2008, _Supplement 1 — Propagation of distributions using a Monte Carlo method_. Clauses 5.3 and 5.5 (coverage intervals, shortest and probabilistically symmetric).
* JCGM 200:2012, _International vocabulary of metrology — Basic and general concepts and associated terms (VIM)_, entries 2.30–2.38.
* UnitsML 2 (numeric contract) and UnitsML 6 (asciiunits content language) of the UnitsML series, for the exact-decimal discipline and the unit embedding.
* PM 1001 (math model) of this series, for the tree and the rendering surfaces.
