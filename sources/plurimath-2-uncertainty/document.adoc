== Status of this draft

Model and the ASCII/MathML/OMML/UnicodeMath representations are implemented (plurimath#495–#509, unreleased until the next plurimath release); this part formalizes them and the remaining surface (plurimath#524).

== Abstract

The uncertainty record: fields (value, category standard/expanded/coverage_interval, magnitude, coverage_factor, coverage_probability, lower/upper, interval_kind shortest/probabilistically_symmetric, notation absolute/concise, unit), exact-decimal text preservation, invariants — a record, never a computation; p never derived from k (JCGM 100 §6.3.2); the magnitude is a DIFFERENCE quantity, so conversion applies the factor and never the offset (UnitsML 4 difference law). Round-trip policy: markers win; the visible form is presentation; read-time disagreement raises `InvalidUncertainty`. Representations, one clause each: MathML 4 `intent` markers (`standard-uncertainty`, `expanded-uncertainty`, `coverage-interval`, `:concise`/`:shortest`/`:probabilistically-symmetric`, `$unit` references); OMML hidden-phantom encoding (risks documented); UnicodeMath `ⓘ("…")` with `〖…〗` negatives and `ⓐ(...)` units; LaTeX/HTML output-only with declared losses. Concise `10.3(2)` is a REPORTING convention (GUM §7.2.2 form 2): output-only; `n((d))` held in reserve; `+-` rejected everywhere (`3+-1` ambiguity); plain `±` remains the Pm symbol.
