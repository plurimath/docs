== MathML 4 — intent markers

Markers ride the standard `intent` mechanism with novel vocabulary:

[listing]
standard-uncertainty(1.230,0.004)
standard-uncertainty:concise(1.230,0.004)
expanded-uncertainty(10.0,0.3,2[,0.95][,$unit])
coverage-interval[:shortest|:probabilistically-symmetric](10.0,9.7,10.3,0.95[,$unit])

Names are NCName-shaped; `:property` lists follow the MathML 4 intent grammar; `$unit` references the `arg="unit"` element. A recognized name with a malformed shape raises; unknown names are left untouched.

[example]
*Expected outcome.* `expanded-uncertainty(10.0,0.3,2,0.95)` decodes to category `expanded`, magnitude `0.3`, k `2`, p `0.95` — the visible `±` beside it is presentation and carries no authority on re-read.
