== Foreword

=== General

This document is the ninth document of the Plurimath specifications series. It specifies the quantity type system: the typed values that carry a number, a unit expression and an optional uncertainty; the unit algebra over SI and scaled units; the dimension vectors that state coherence; and the typed errors that make every violation a diagnostic instead of a silent wrong answer.

The system is the semantic plane's foundation. PM 1004's evaluator binds ordinary real numbers and refuses everything else; this document defines what the evaluator binds from the semantic edition onwards — quantities whose arithmetic delegates to the unit algebra and whose numbers obey the accuracy-class discipline of PM 1004, clause 7.
