        == The typed errors

        === The diagnostics as data

        The quantity algebra's failures are diagnostic values, constructed with their identifying data and carried like results. At the evaluation boundary (PM 1011) they raise as the corresponding typed errors; in construction and reduction they are the returned diagnostic. There is no silent wrong answer.

        === The errors

.The quantity errors
[cols="2,4", options="headers"]
|===
|Error |Carries

|`DimensionMismatch` |`expected`, `got` — the two dimension vectors
|`UnitNotConvertible` |`from`, `to` — the two unit expressions
|`UnknownUnit` |`code` — the unregistered symbol
|`AffineUnitInAlgebra` |`unit`, `operation` — the affine unit and the operation refused
|===

        === The corpus

        The corpus carries one vector per error per constructible site: construction, reduction, comparison, conversion and (in PM 1011) evaluation. The diagnostic's carried data is asserted in the vector, not just its class.
