== Scope

This document specifies:

* the quantity value: the typed pair of a number (with its accuracy class per PM 1004) and a unit expression, with an optional uncertainty in the model of PM 1002;
* the unit expression algebra: SI base and derived units, scaled units, compound units, and the affine scales handled explicitly;
* the dimension vectors: the reduction of every unit expression to its dimensions and the coherence rules that consume them;
* the conversion contract: the registered factors and offsets, and the affine traps;
* the typed errors of the quantity algebra, stated as data.

This document does not specify:

* the uncertainty model itself (PM 1002) — the uncertainty field references it;
* evaluation and propagation over quantities (PM 1011);
* the numeric contract of exact decimals in the data (CalConnect 36813) — this document binds numbers whose accuracy class is stated, under PM 1004's algebra;
* the expression syntax that constructs quantities (PM 1010).
