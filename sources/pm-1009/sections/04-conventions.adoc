== Conventions

=== General

Quantities are written as number, space, unit: `3 kV`. Unit expressions are written in the ordinary notation (`kg/m^3`, `V·A`, `kW`) with `^` for integer powers and `/` for quotients.

=== Examples

Examples carry an *Expected outcome* paragraph stating the constructed quantity, the reduced dimension, or the typed error with its data.

=== Units

Unit names in examples are the SI spellings and symbols; the authority reference is the UnitsML code, written `unitsml(m)` where the code matters.
