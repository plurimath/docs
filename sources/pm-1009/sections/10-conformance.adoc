== Conformance

=== The conformance class

An implementation conforms to this document when it constructs, reduces, compares and converts quantity values exactly as clauses 5 to 8 state, produces the typed errors of clause 9 with their carried data, and replays the corpus vectors of this document identically.

=== The acceptance vectors

* `kV·A` reduces to the power dimension at the kilo scale;
* `3 km` compares to `2500 m` after conversion, exactly;
* `3 m` and `2 s` refuse with `DimensionMismatch` carrying both vectors;
* the affine traps (difference vs absolute) return the stated answers, and the corpus pins them;
* an unregistered symbol refuses with `UnknownUnit` naming the code.

=== The corpus

The vectors of this document are the quantity rows of the UnitsML testsuite. A claim names the repository, the commit and the generation below; an implementation replayed against a different commit makes a different claim.

=== Known gaps at the pinned commit

* The `quantity-register` family is partial: its rows are produced by the native evaluator, and an independent exact check covers 16 of its 32 identifiers.
* No source names the quantity class of `kV·A` (apparent against active power). The corpus states the reduction to the power dimension and the conversion to `kW` by dimension only; it asserts no class. Asserting or refusing a class is an open decision of this document.
* The UnitsML Ruby `Quantity#==` compares the raw value and the dimension vector without converting the scale (`10 kV * 5 A == 50 W` is true; corpus row `kva-equals-50-W-upstream-spec` and the file's `source_semantics_findings`). Clause 5 ("Equality and comparison") requires conversion to a common scale before comparison, so that row records a non-conformance of the implementation, not an acceptance vector.
* No row yet pins the comparison `3 km` against `2500 m`, the reductions of `mm·kN` and `V/Hz`, or a quantity carrying a PM 1002 uncertainty node.
