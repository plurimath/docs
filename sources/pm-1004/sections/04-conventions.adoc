== Conventions

=== General

Evaluations are written as: tree, bindings, result. Errors are written as their class names of clause 11 with the identifying data the message carries.

=== Examples

Examples carry an *Expected outcome* paragraph stating the number yielded or the error raised. Numbers in examples are exact where they are exact: a rational binding such as one third behaves as its exact value in every example that uses it.

=== Names

Names in examples (`x`, `n`, `i`) are the plain identifiers the trees carry. The mapping from a tree's symbols to names is pinned by the corpus; this document treats names as the keys of the binding set.
