== Scope

=== General

This document specifies evaluation of Plurimath formula trees:

* the evaluator: its inputs, its contract, and the shape of its results;
* the expression parser: the precedence that turns a tree's linear reading into operations;
* the arithmetic: every operation, its domain, and its exactness;
* functions and their arguments;
* bindings: keys, values, and the errors they raise;
* bounded iteration for the nary sum and product;
* the error contract: the eight typed errors and what each one means;
* conformance of an implementation.

This document does not specify:

* uncertainty propagation, combination or coverage computation — not implemented in this edition (see clause 12);
* unit conversion or quantity arithmetic — the UnitsML series governs units, and evaluation works on numbers;
* simplification, factorisation or any rewriting — the tree is evaluated as written;
* the numeric contract of exact decimals in the data — UnitsML 2 governs that, and this evaluator binds ordinary real numbers.
