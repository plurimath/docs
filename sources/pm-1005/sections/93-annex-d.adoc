=== Tokens with several spellings

Some entries accept more than one token: the arrows carry their ASCII spellings (`->`, `-->`) alongside their named ones; the logic tokens carry words (`and`, `or`) alongside signs. Every spelling reads to the same entry and writes to the entry's canonical AsciiMath token — the round trip may re-spell, and the tree does not change.

=== What is not a symbol

Digits, the structure characters, the construct names, the font names and the quoted strings are not symbol tokens; the reader's classification is total and a token belongs to exactly one class. The boundary cases are decided by the constants and pinned by the corpus: `e` alone is a symbol; `e5` is nothing (a reading error); `2e5` is a number followed by an error.
