== Scope

=== General

This document specifies the AsciiMath notation as implemented by Plurimath:

* the token surface: symbols, numbers, function names, fonts, fences;
* the construct shapes: unary, binary, ternary and sub/sup classes, with their arguments;
* tables and their bracket forms;
* scripts and fractions, with the precedence that joins them;
* quoted strings and the UnitsML channel;
* the uncertainty forms of PM 1002 as they read and write in AsciiMath;
* precedence and grouping;
* the reading algorithm and its errors;
* the corpus and conformance.

This document does not specify: the other formats (PM 1003), the tree (PM 1001), the uncertainty model (PM 1002), evaluation (PM 1004), or the pma notation, which is its own document.

=== Compatibility with asciimath.org

Plurimath's AsciiMath descends from the asciimath.org public definition and extends it. This document specifies Plurimath's surface; where it extends or sharpens the public definition, the clause says so in a *Compatibility note*. The compatibility notes are informational; the rules are normative.

=== Audience

Implementers of Plurimath and of other AsciiMath processors, authors writing AsciiMath, and tool builders.
