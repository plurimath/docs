Generations extend by appending; a generation's entries are immutable once published, and the digest replay covers all generations on every run.

=== Entry provenance

Each entry names its origin: the clause of this document that first required it, the defect it froze, or the report that brought it. The provenance is the specification's institutional memory: a behaviour with a provenance entry is deliberate, and a proposal to change it must name the entry.

=== Where the corpus lives

The corpus is versioned outside this document, pinned by the replay script the implementation carries. This document's claims name the generation they were checked against; the register's build checks the naming.
