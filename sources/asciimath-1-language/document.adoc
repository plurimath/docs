== Status of this draft

The language is implemented (the amath PARG artifact, generated from the shared registry) and corpus-frozen; this part formalizes it.

== Abstract

The asciimath compatibility dialect: the language surface (numbers; unary/binary operators; fractions, roots, scripts; the `legacy`-tagged symbol vocabulary from the shared registry) under the compatibility contract — every asciimath.org construct parses and renders with unchanged meaning; the closed deviation ledger (lenient fallback symbols; locale decimal rejection via `AmbiguousDecimalError`; nothing else, ever); the strict absence list (no uncertainty syntax, no `unit(...)` constructor, no measured literals — uncertainty rides the quoted channel of Part 2); and materialization — parse trees map through the registry's primitive bindings into the Plurimath math model (Plurimath 1). The checksummed artifact is the normative grammar.
