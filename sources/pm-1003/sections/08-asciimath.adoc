== The AsciiMath interface

=== Reading

The AsciiMath reader accepts the linear form: symbols and function applications of the registry vocabulary (PM 1001), grouping, scripts, fractions, nary constructions, text in `text(...)` and in quoted strings, and the uncertainty forms of PM 1002.

The quoted string is content: `"unitsml(kg*m/s^2)"` is the UnitsML channel, read as a unit embedding; any other quoted string is upright text. The reader does not interpret inside a quoted string beyond the UnitsML channel check.

The uncertainty reading follows PM 1002:

* the calls `ustd(v, m)`, `uexp(v, m, k, p)` and `ucov(v, l, u, p, kind)` read to their categories with the given fields;
* the reporting form `v(digits)` reads to a standard uncertainty with notation concise and the magnitude reconstructed by the decimal-places rule;
* the interval kinds spell `shortest` and `symmetric` on input, reading to the model's kinds;
* a malformed form — wrong arity, non-decimal arguments, an unknown kind — is a reading error: `AmbiguousDecimalError` where the ambiguity is the decimal place count, `ParseError` otherwise.

=== Writing

The AsciiMath writer emits per PM 1002's encodings: the calls with their argument orders, the concise reporting form for concise nodes, the quoted UnitsML channel for expressible units and the visible unit otherwise.

=== Losslessness

The AsciiMath interface is lossless: writing a tree and reading the result yields an equal tree, for every tree the model defines. The one deliberate asymmetry: a concise node writes the reporting form and reads back concise — the notation survives the round trip, as a field of the node.

[example]
Writing `ustd(10.3, 0.2)` and reading it back; writing the same node with notation concise and reading that back.
*Expected outcome.* the first round trip returns the node with notation absolute; the second returns notation concise. The two trees differ in that field and in nothing else.

=== Precedence and grouping

The reader applies the notation's precedence: scripts bind tighter than adjacency, fractions tighter than addition, and explicit grouping overrides. The tree is the precedence-correct reading; an author who means something else parenthesises. This document does not restate the precedence table — the reader's behaviour is its definition, and the corpus pins it vector by vector.

=== Localization

The localized symbols and function names follow the active locale: an Italian locale reads `sen` as the sine function where an English reading sees a symbol sequence. Localization affects which tokens map to which nodes; it never changes the tree shape a construct yields.

=== Errors and the corpus

The reader's refusals are pinned by the corpus alongside its successes: every reading error of Annex A that the AsciiMath surface can produce has a corpus vector whose expected outcome is the error and its message shape.

=== The quoted channel, precisely

A quoted string in AsciiMath input is content, with exactly one special case: text matching the UnitsML channel shape `unitsml(<expression>)` becomes a unit embedding. The check is on the whole string, after trimming nothing: `" unitsml(g)"` with a leading space is text, `"unitsml(g)"` is the channel. Anything inside the channel that the unit reader refuses is a reading error naming the expression — the channel never silently degrades to text, because a unit intended as a unit must not become prose.

=== Concise reconstruction, precisely

On reading the reporting form `v(digits)`:

. the value is the text before the parenthesis;
. the digit string is the text inside;
. the magnitude's decimal places are the value's decimal places — the count that made the form well formed;
. the magnitude is the digit string with its last digit(s) moved behind a decimal point at that place count, prefixed with `0.` when the place count is positive;
. the node is constructed with notation concise, through PM 1002's validation.

A digit string that cannot be reconstructed at the value's place count — more digits than places is unambiguous, fewer or mismatched is not — raises `AmbiguousDecimalError`. The reconstruction never invents places: `10(2)` reads magnitude `2` at zero places; `10.3(25)` refuses.

=== Symmetric, precisely

The AsciiMath kind token `symmetric` reads to `probabilistically-symmetric` before node construction; the model never sees the short spelling. Writing a `probabilistically-symmetric` node emits `symmetric` in AsciiMath and the full name everywhere else. The asymmetry is spell-level and pinned in both corpora.

=== Writing matrices and alignment

The table classes write in the bracket forms; `Align` and `Eqarray` write with the ampersand alignment their reads accept. Alignment points are structural for these classes and survive the round trip; a writer that drops them fails the corpus.

=== Text and quotes, precisely

`text(...)` reads its content as upright text with grouping; quoted strings read as text with the UnitsML channel exception of this clause. The two forms differ in one observable way: `text(...)` may contain AsciiMath commands, a quoted string never does — quotes are literal, which is what makes them the right channel for content a formula must not interpret.

=== What the corpus pins for AsciiMath

The AsciiMath corpora carry, at minimum: every registry family with a representative per entry-group; every construct family of the table above, in plain and grouped forms; the uncertainty forms of PM 1002 with all categories, notations and kinds; the quoted channel with expressible, inexpressible and refused units; the localization pairs; and the error table's rows. 143 cases formed the first frozen generation; later generations extend, never rewrite.
